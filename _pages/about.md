Hyunjeong Kwak ([hear my name](https://namedrop.io/hjkwak)) is a Ph.D. student in Sociology at the University of Maryland, College Park. Her research asks how people interpret situations in which care and constraint become difficult to distinguish.

Originally trained in social network analysis, she is especially interested in the emotional and moral-psychological processes through which people negotiate expectations and develop identities in relation to others. Her current projects focus on intimate relationships and couple dynamics, but her broader work bridges social psychology, cultural sociology, and network research to understand how relationships shape well-being, decision-making, and inequality.

Before joining UMD, she earned her M.A. in Sociology at Yonsei University, where she studied intimate relationships and social support across the life course. Her work is ultimately concerned with why some forms of constraint feel ordinary, why others become morally troubling, and how people decide whether to accept or challenge them.
